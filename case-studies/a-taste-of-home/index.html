Food · UGC · ILLUSTRATIVE CASE STUDY

Good food. Even better stories.

A sample content programme bringing a pantry essential into real kitchens.

18 creators1.4M views96K engagements

Example figures only. This campaign has not been represented as completed client work.

The challenge

Make a familiar pantry product feel useful and fresh for home cooks.

The approach

Regional food creators build simple recipes around the product. The content can be adapted for social, paid ads, and product pages with agreed usage rights.

What we would measure

Views and engagement across agreed formats, audience relevance, content quality, and any tracked campaign traffic. Real results would need source analytics, a clear reporting window, and client approval before publication.
