A LITTLE MORE CLARITY

Good questions. Clear answers.

How do we get started?

Begin with a campaign brief: your goal, audience, platform, and budget. The demo builder saves these details locally. A production intake service will be connected before real enquiries can be submitted.

How are creators selected?

Creator selection should consider audience relevance, content style, location, language, engagement, and agreed deliverables. The sample directory demonstrates that workflow with fictional profiles.

Can we use creator content in paid ads?

Usage rights, platforms, duration, and paid distribution should be agreed with each creator before production. They are part of a clear campaign scope, not automatically included.
